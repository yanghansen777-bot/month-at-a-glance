WHY IT BELONGS IN SCHOOL

Turn screen time into
creative time.

Students do more than play. They plan a concept, build a world, test what they make, and explain their decisions. That makes the day engaging while giving educators a visible learning process.

A concern, answered: Build Day is designed for a supervised school setting. Educators can group students, set the pace, and use the provided checkpoints to keep the day focused and age-appropriate.

WHAT STUDENTS PRACTICE

Clear learning goals,
made tangible.

01

Design thinking

Students move from a rough idea to a testable game concept.

02

Problem-solving

They identify what is not working, revise it, and try again.

03

Digital creation

They learn foundational Roblox Studio skills and basic game logic.

04

Communication

They share their process, give feedback, and present what they made.

A SIMPLE SCHOOL-DAY STRUCTURE

Built for a classroom,
club, or special event.

  1. PrepareEducators introduce the challenge and form small teams.
  2. BuildStudents follow guided milestones with time for experimentation.
  3. ReflectTeams test, improve, and share what they learned.
